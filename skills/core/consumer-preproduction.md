# Shared Consumer Preproduction

Use the same strategy contracts for product promos, faceless narration, and
two-host dialogue. Format changes delivery, not the standard of evidence.
This skill is required before every research, proposal, script, or scene stage
in the faceless and dialogue pipelines.

## Research

Read these directors under `skills/pipelines/product-promo-factory/`:
`product-truth-director.md`, `pain-research-director.md`,
`audience-job-director.md`, and `value-director.md`.
Persist their existing schemas, not parallel lookalikes:
`product_truth`, `pain_library`, `audience_job`, and `value_map`, alongside
`research_brief`. For non-product topics, treat the subject as the fact boundary
and use a learning/action CTA; do not invent a commercial product.

Verify the real input-to-result journey. Distinguish website claims, observed
behavior, inference, and blocked/unverified steps. Record dates, source URLs,
asset ownership, and disallowed claims. No unsupported traffic, sales, or search
volume estimates. Demand rankings without analytics are hypotheses.

Classify each entry's `kind` as `consumer_need`, `product_defect`, or
`production_blocker`. Only consumer needs can be selected as ad angles. Keep
site defects in the engineering backlog, even when they are urgent. Set
unmeasured frequency to `null`, not a fabricated demand score.

For a campaign, explore 20-50 distinct needs, then draft about ten scripts and
recommend five. For a single sample, investigate several needs, propose three
hooks, and lock one consumer problem before obtaining media.

## Proposal

Read the factory `hook-director.md`, `creative-director.md`,
`proposal-director.md`, and `production-contract-director.md`.
Produce `hook_candidates`, `creative_brief`, `proposal_packet`,
`production_preset`, and append-only `decision_log`. Dialogue also needs
`cast_bible`. Present the available runtimes and explicit voice, music, motion,
budget, and authorization boundaries. Do not mark user approval yourself.

Select one consumer, one promise, and one action. Reject a direction when its
core promise has no verifiable visual proof. A missing provider is a blocker,
not permission to replace real motion with stills or polished speech with
system narration.

## Script

Read the factory `script-director.md` and `script-quality-director.md`.
The first three seconds must
communicate the viewer's problem. Then show a solution, concrete proof, and one
CTA. Narration and dialogue use the same claim IDs. A skeptical host is the
viewer advocate, not a fake customer testimonial.

Persist `script_qa_report` together with `script` (and `dialogue_script`).
Label word-count timing as an estimate; only an actual recording/TTS sample
can supply measured duration. Do not set `measured: true` from word counts.
Block unsupported claims and missing hook payoff before asset generation.

## Scene Plan

Read the factory `proof-director.md`, `continuity-director.md`, and
`shotcraft-director.md`. Persist
`proof_plan`, `visual_continuity_bible`, and `shot_language_plan` alongside
`scene_plan`. Each shot links the spoken claim to an actual proof source,
composition, movement, transition, and audio purpose. Cite Shotcraft recipes
only when their mechanics are implemented and inspected.

Product output is proof; stock footage is context. Never use stock or generated
mockups as an unlabeled successful product result. Keep one visual identity,
avoid decorative transitions that hide the interface, and reserve readable
space for subtitles and the CTA.

## Acceptance

Use the approved production preset for resource limits and output formats.
Bind creative review to the final media and inputs, inspect image and sound,
then evaluate the report. Decoder success is not creative approval. A new
render or changed input needs a fresh review. Preserve every failed attempt;
stop at the retry limit without waiving critical findings.
