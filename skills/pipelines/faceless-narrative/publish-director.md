# Faceless Narrative Publish Director

This pipeline publishes locally by default. Use `export_bundle` to create the
canonical `publish_log` and a self-contained delivery bundle.

Include final MP4, poster/contact sheet, narration script, hook candidates,
captions, research sources, asset provenance, editable project, render report,
and final review. Label the format, language, duration, aspect ratio, hook ID, and
revision. Do not claim platform publication or performance without evidence.
