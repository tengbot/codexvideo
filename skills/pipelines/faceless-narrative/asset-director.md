# Faceless Narrative Asset Director

Produce `asset_manifest`, `visual_match`, and `audio_timeline`.

Generate narration first so measured timing becomes scene and subtitle truth.
Search or generate at least two candidates for important scenes when practical.
Score relevance, composition, motion, and brand fit; select the highest useful
candidate, not the prettiest generic clip.

Record provider, creator, source URL, license, generation prompt/model, and local
path. Preserve first-party captures separately from stock and generated media.
Reject candidates below 3/5 relevance. If search fails, revise the visual intent
or use designed motion graphics instead of accepting atmosphere-only footage.

Keep music instrumental and subordinate to speech. Produce word timings or an
aligned transcript and ensure all referenced files exist before approval.
