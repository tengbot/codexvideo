# Faceless Narrative Proposal Director

First read `skills/core/consumer-preproduction.md`. All outputs declared by the
stage manifest are required, including the shared consumer and proof contracts.

Read the research brief and create `proposal_packet`, `hook_candidates`, and an
append-only `decision_log`.

Generate at least three hooks rooted in different human needs. Score each for
truth, retention, and relevance. The selected hook must be supported by named
evidence and must promise a payoff that appears in the video.

Present both Remotion and HyperFrames when both are available. Describe each in
plain language for this brief, recommend one, and wait for approval in guided
mode. Record the choice as `render_runtime_selection`; `render_runtime` must be
locked in the proposal. Store the runtime value as `remotion` or `hyperframes`.
Also record composition mode, narration provider/voice,
visual acquisition plan, target duration, aspect ratio, and cost ceiling.

Do not start assets during proposal. An attractive moodboard is not evidence that
the hook works.
