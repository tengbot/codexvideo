# AI Dialogue Podcast Proposal Director

Create `proposal_packet`, `hook_candidates`, `cast_bible`, and `decision_log`.

Generate at least three conversational hooks. A hook can be a challenge,
surprising question, contradiction, or decision problem, but it must be grounded
in research. Score truth, retention, and relevance before selection.

Define two durable host roles, voice directions, avatar sources, studio rules,
wardrobe, eyelines, and primary camera framing. Present both Remotion and
HyperFrames when both are available and record the approved choice as
`render_runtime_selection`; lock `render_runtime` in the proposal. Also record the
runtime value as `remotion` or `hyperframes`. Also record the avatar provider
path, composition mode, duration, aspect ratio, budget, and the
planned ratio of close-ups, two-shots, reactions, and evidence inserts.

Do not approve a path that lacks a real avatar-generation capability without
labelling the output as a degraded prototype.
