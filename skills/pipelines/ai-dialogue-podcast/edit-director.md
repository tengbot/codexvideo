# AI Dialogue Podcast Edit Director

Produce canonical `edit_decisions` and supplementary `editable_timeline`.

Cut on conversational intent and reaction, not on every word. Keep the active
speaker visible when mouth movement matters; use listener shots only when their
mouth remains neutral. Use two-shots to establish space and punctuate shared
moments. Evidence inserts should answer the current line and return before the
conversation loses momentum.

Mix both voices to consistent perceived loudness, keep room tone subtle, and duck
music under speech. Captions identify speakers when visually ambiguous and stay
clear of faces. Preserve stable element IDs and per-turn regeneration metadata in
the editable timeline.
