# Shotcraft Director

Convert the approved proof plan into a traceable shot-language plan before final
asset capture. Resolve every selected card through `shotcraft_catalog`; do not
implement a card from its name or summary alone. Read the complete recipe and the
exact referenced demo TSX, then record both paths in `shot_language_plan`.

Use one primary motion idea per scene. Match the card to the scene's proof purpose,
energy, duration, and required product state. A shot may dramatize a pain or focus
attention, but it may not replace first-party product evidence with decorative UI.
Do not repeat the same hero technique more than twice in a short promo.

Plan the native destination aspect ratio. For 9:16, recompose the subject and camera
path rather than center-cropping a 16:9 demo. Keep critical text and controls inside
the platform safe area, define at least two acceptance frames per scene, and specify
the exact full-page texture, element cutouts, backplates, or layout coordinates that
the Asset Director must capture.

Pin SFX to relative scene frames only after the visual timing is defined. Use only
audio with verified commercial provenance. Preserve the vendored Apache-2.0 license
and mark adapted source files.
