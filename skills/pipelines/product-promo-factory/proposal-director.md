# Product Promo Factory Proposal Director

Read the approved `creative_brief` and live capability preflight before creating
`proposal_packet` and the append-only `decision_log`. Do not generate paid assets
or begin composition in this stage.

Present both Remotion and HyperFrames when both runtimes are available. For each,
describe in plain language what it is best at for this specific promotion and one
honest tradeoff. Recommend one, explain why it fits the approved proof strategy and
delivery promise, and wait for approval in guided mode. Record the result as
`render_runtime_selection`; lock `render_runtime` to `remotion` or `hyperframes`.
Include FFmpeg only when a footage-led treatment can satisfy the approved promise
without designed motion. Never silently reduce a motion-led concept to FFmpeg.

Present templated and atelier as a separate composition-mode decision. Recommend
atelier for a hero launch piece that must look unique; recommend templated for fast,
repeatable campaign variants. Record both options and the approved choice in the
decision log.

The proposal must also name the intended voice, media acquisition path, generation
providers and model variants, sample-before-batch plan, target duration, aspect
ratio, delivery profiles, cost estimate, and hard cost ceiling. Every provider is a
proposal, not an implicit default. A blocked provider or runtime requires a new
approved decision entry rather than an unlogged substitution.
