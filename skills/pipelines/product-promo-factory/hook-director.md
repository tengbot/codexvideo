# Hook Director

Write at least five hooks per selected audience job across distinct patterns: direct
pain, failed alternative, risk or loss, before and after, proof-led, objection
dialogue, desire-led, or contrarian. Start from the approved consumer situation and
desired progress, not a feature or a clever phrase.

Each hook must include `pain_id`, `hook_type`, `first_frame`, evidence references,
and measured or estimated speech duration. Version 1.1 hooks also require a credibility
basis, a body-payoff plan, and an English speakability check. Credibility references
must already exist in the candidate's evidence references; do not invent data,
authority, testimonials, or results to make an opening stronger.

Run `promo_strategy evaluate_hooks`. Only hooks with an explicit consumer pain, no
brand-first opening, synchronized first frame, matching body payoff, supported
credibility, and duration at or below three seconds may be selected. A hook is a sample
of the promised body: reject it when the script cannot honestly deliver what it opens.
