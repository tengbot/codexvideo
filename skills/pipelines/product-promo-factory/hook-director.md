# Hook Director

Write at least five hooks per selected pain across distinct patterns: direct pain,
failed alternative, risk or loss, before and after, proof-led, objection dialogue,
desire-led, or contrarian.

Each hook must include `pain_id`, `hook_type`, `first_frame`, evidence references,
and measured or estimated speech duration. Run `promo_strategy evaluate_hooks`.
Only hooks with an explicit consumer pain, no brand-first opening, synchronized first
frame, and duration at or below three seconds may be selected.
