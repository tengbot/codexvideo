# Product Truth Director

Capture the current public product, its primary workflows, pricing or offer claims,
and material limitations. Write `product_truth.json` before promotional copy.

Every claim needs a first-party source reference and one of `verified`, `qualified`,
or `unverified`. Never promote an unverified claim. Store screenshots or captured
pages as evidence, not merely as decoration. Phrase `consumer_value` in the user's
language while keeping the factual `statement` precise.
