# Pain Research Director

Build 20-50 distinct pains from search intent, reviews, communities, competitor
comments, support questions, and product friction. Preserve the user's wording and
evidence reference for every pain.

Score frequency, urgency, severity, commercial intent, and evidence confidence from
zero to five. Run `promo_strategy rank_pains`; do not hand-pick a favorite before the
ranking exists. Select at most five pains for the next campaign.
