# Audience Job Director

Translate each selected pain into an evidence-linked consumer progress profile before
writing promises or hooks. Start from the consumer's current situation, the progress
they are trying to make, and the criteria they can actually observe when choosing a
solution. Do not begin with product features.

Create one profile per selected pain unless the evidence shows that several pains are
different expressions of the same job. Record functional, emotional, and social needs
separately. Record the forces pushing the consumer away from the current situation,
pulling them toward a new solution, making them anxious about switching, and keeping
them with the familiar alternative. Empty dimensions are allowed; invented evidence is
not.

Every profile must cite the pain or source evidence that supports it. Put uncertain
interpretations in `unknowns`, lower `confidence`, and preserve the uncertainty. Choice
criteria must be observable, such as time to first result, visible output quality, price,
privacy behavior, or required setup. Approve at most five profiles for a campaign.
