# Asset Director

Source only assets required by the proof plan. Track provider, URL or local path,
license, scene, and claim reference. The selected first frame must make the pain
legible before sound is understood.

Read `shot_language_plan` before capture. Collect the exact full-page textures,
high-resolution focus regions, element cutouts, empty backplates, and layout
coordinates required by each selected recipe. Do not substitute a low-resolution
whole-page screenshot for a planned crash zoom or 2.5D close-up.

Generate natural English narration for English markets, word timings, captions, and
a restrained music bed. Reject static screenshot slideshows: product screens need
purposeful camera movement, focus, highlighting, or interaction.
